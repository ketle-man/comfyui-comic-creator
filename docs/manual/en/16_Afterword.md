# Afterword

Thank you for reading to the end.

Comic Creator began with a simple wish: "I want to turn pictures made in ComfyUI straight into manga pages." I made it possible to lay out panels, add balloons and dialogue, and export, all on one screen, and then gradually added an image editor and AI integrations.

This book has walked you from your first page through AI-assisted creation to finishing effects. You do not need to master every feature at once. Start with something small, such as adding text to a single image or making a short two-page manga.

Comic Creator is still being updated. If the screen differs from this book, please also check the in-app "Help" tab. Bug reports and feature requests are welcome in the GitHub repository.

I hope Comic Creator helps you bring your works to life.
